About this data

An LCA is a filing, not proof of a hire. Certification does not mean a person was employed, or that a job was ever posted.

Source: U.S. Department of Labor, Office of Foreign Labor Certification case disclosure data, fiscal years 2024–2026. dol.gov/agencies/eta/foreign-labor/performance

DOL publishes quarterly, so recent filings may not appear yet. This tool reports what employers filed. It does not predict whether anyone will be hired.
